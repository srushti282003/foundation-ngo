Volunteer FAQ

Answers to common questions about volunteering with Abhigam Welfare Foundation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Anyone who is passionate about community welfare and willing to contribute their time and skills. We welcome volunteers of all ages and backgrounds.

There is no fixed minimum commitment. You can volunteer for a few hours, a day or on an ongoing basis depending on your availability and the activities available.

We welcome volunteers with any skills — teaching, healthcare, communication, photography, digital skills or even just enthusiasm and willingness to help. All backgrounds are valued.

Visit our Volunteer page and fill out the registration form. Your details will be shared via WhatsApp with our team who will get in touch with you shortly.

Volunteers receive a brief orientation about our initiatives and activities. Specific guidance is provided based on the activity you are involved in.

Yes, some activities such as digital communication, design, documentation and social media support can be done remotely. Contact us to discuss remote volunteering options.

No. Volunteering with Abhigam Welfare Foundation is completely free. We only ask for your time and dedication.
