Education Initiative

Supporting learning and creating opportunities for children in underserved communities.

EDUCATION

Learning Support for Children

Abhigam Welfare Foundation's education initiative focuses on ensuring that children from underserved communities have access to quality learning resources and support.

We work to bridge learning gaps, encourage school attendance and support academic progress through community-based programmes.

Get Involved

What We Do

Learning Support

Providing study support, materials and guidance to children who need it most.

Resource Access

Connecting children with books, stationery and educational resources.

Community Engagement

Encouraging family and community involvement in children's education.

Awareness Programmes

Building awareness about the importance of education for all children.

Child-Centred Activities

Organising activities that make learning enjoyable and engaging.

Community Partnerships

Collaborating with local schools and community centres.
